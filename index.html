Which constitutions outlive the generation that wrote them?

I work on constitutional mechanism design: the formal conditions under which a constitution stays worth obeying once the people who ratified it are gone, and what becomes of a republic when those conditions fail.

Research

Classical political theory says regimes turn in a circle. Polybius called it anacyclosis: monarchy decays into tyranny, aristocracy into oligarchy, democracy into mob rule, and the wheel comes round. Modern political economy says institutions are chosen, by people who can see the wheel coming.

My work asks when both hold at once: when the cycle is an equilibrium object, and what a constitution would have to look like to step off it. The formal apparatus is axiomatic social choice and dynamical systems. The empirical setting is Latin America, where constitutions are rewritten often enough that constitutional mortality is observable.

00

Anacyclosis as a Hopf bifurcation

Regime cycling recovered as a limit cycle in a dynamic political economy model, with the speed of the demographic transition as the bifurcation parameter. Below the threshold the polity spirals into a stable constitutional equilibrium. Above it, that equilibrium loses stability and the regime sequence turns periodic. Validated against V-Dem.

01

A constitutional impossibility theorem

Five axioms (stability, non-capture, openness, commitment efficiency, anonymity) are shown to be mutually incompatible in an overlapping-generations economy with heterogeneous patience and multidimensional policy. Epistocratic bicameralism is characterised as the unique escape. A companion convergence result shows the constitution self-obsolesces as the patience distribution shifts.

02

Constitutional mortality in Latin America

The causal companion. Regression discontinuity around constitutional reform thresholds, using close elections and the bifurcation threshold itself as cutoffs, testing whether the cycling predicted by 00 shows up as a discontinuity in constitutional survival.

Two further papers sit at memo stage: a torus paper on quasi-periodic constitutional dynamics, embedding the impossibility result of 01 in the cycling of 00, and a quantum social choice extension built on a novel intergenerational consistency axiom.
