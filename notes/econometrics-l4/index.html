OLS estimator and finite-sample properties

The OLS formula, unbiasedness under OLS.1–3, variance under OLS.1–4, Gauss–Markov, and the residual maker.

All notes

1. The linear model and the OLS estimator

The model is y = Xβ + u. OLS is β̂ = (X'X)-1X'y. Residuals are orthogonal to every column of X. It minimises the sum of squared residuals when X has full column rank.

Takeaway

Linear means linear in parameters. Curved relationships between y and the data are allowed as long as the parameters enter linearly.

2. Finite-sample properties

Under OLS.1–3 (linearity, full rank, strict exogeneity) β̂ is unbiased. Under OLS.1–4 (plus spherical errors) its variance is σ²(X'X)-1. The Gauss–Markov theorem says it is the best linear unbiased estimator.

3. Residual maker and fitted values

The residual maker M = I - P projects onto the orthogonal complement of the column space of X. Fitted values are Py, residuals are My, and M is idempotent and symmetric.
